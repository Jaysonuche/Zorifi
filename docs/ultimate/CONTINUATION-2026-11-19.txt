Audit found WAV export only visited root clips, ignored explicit composition
length, used mutating ensure(), omitted category gain, and caught/ignored source
decode failures. It could present an incomplete mix as a successful download.
zorifi-offline-mix.js now derives exact root intervals/source offsets from affine
positive constant-rate nested clocks, validates them against the native graph,
uses read-only normalization and existing source processing, and applies inherited
private-track/container gain/pan plus source fades/volume/pan/ducking and mixer
category/master controls. No alternate editor store or server/provider is used.
The exact composition duration includes initial/trailing silence. Samples, WAV
export and the bounded preflight share this renderer; nested audio is measured
when supported instead of a misleading root-only silent result.

Mix limits:600s range;64 audible layers;64MiB encoded/source,128MiB encoded total;
256MiB decoded total and128MiB stereo output;1.2million graph automation workload.
Preflight lowers source/encoded budgets to8 layers/40MiB/source/64MiB total and
samples the first12s at16kHz. Source reads are bounded while streaming, decoded
originals are cached per source and output nodes/buffers disconnected/released.
decodeAudioData still decodes entire originals and cannot be natively aborted;
decoded memory is checked after decoding. This is not unlimited-media support.
WebAudio source rates change pitch.50Hz gain/pan automation is an approximation,
not sample-accurate arbitrary automation or pitch-preserving time stretch.
Variable-rate/remapped audio, expression-driven leaf gain/pan and non-default
container DSP/fades are rejected explicitly. They are not silently flattened.
Stereo sample peaks/RMS/full-scale counts are real PCM metrics, not true-peak/LUFS.

Any failed eligible source stops the whole export. Stop, modal close, page hide,
account/project/revision/source changes prevent late downloads/status/save and
remove private export UI. Native decode/render may finish underneath cancellation;
results are discarded. Download URLs are revoked on close/account change.
