11 November 2026 | Source: attached master specification, sections 1–29.

This continues the existing application and commits ac099cf/5a7d286. It does not
replace the ten-item editing report with a claim that the full commercial master
specification is finished. Implemented/Tests below refer to bounded supported
paths, not universal creative quality, every codec or physical device.
The current release stage repairs and verifies native editing, quota recovery,
manual/Vanessa parity and export. It does not activate new payment collection,
OpenAI production use, paid infrastructure or unapproved prices/allowances.
